## Status Legend
- **IMPLEMENTED**: Complete DB, backend API, RBAC, frontend UI, and tests verified.
- **PARTIAL**: Core or adjacent foundation exists in codebase; expansion required for full v1.2 parity.
- **MISSING**: Not yet implemented; scheduled for Phases 2–14.
- **CONFLICT**: Client wording or assumption conflicts with previously approved architecture or awaits formal sign-off.
- **OUT_OF_SCOPE**: Excluded third-party integration per Master Prompt Section 2.
